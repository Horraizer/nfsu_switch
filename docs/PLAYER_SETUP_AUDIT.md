## Normal runtime aero

57F2B0 clears RideInfo+2C/+30 before575960. At57F579 it adds continuous
front/rear downforce and suspension preload coefficients from the authored
maximum aero rows, scaled by(weight+suspension+tyres)/9. Its x87 control
word007F rounds each operation to binary32. Cosmetic skirt/spoiler levels
are not independent normal-race physics table selections in this wrapper.
They remain available to the raw apply_vehicle_tuning table API.

make_race_vehicle_spec now runs this normal runtime policy. Adaptive rivals
consume the same producer and do not add the continuous term a second time.
The separate drift transformation is outside this normal-race correction;
this document does not assert full drift or whole-race trajectory parity.

The fixture tests/race_setup_aero_native.inc is generated by executing
original57F2B0 with tools/race_setup_aero_native.py, without semantic hooks.
It covers both240SX and Civic across every attainable sum of the three
handling levels. nfsu_performance_levels_tests exercises their combinations,
visual-level independence and the force/preload consumer. Numeric pins live
in the fixture and test, not here.

## Other installed components

career_performance_levels maps the saved nine performance fields and the
unique-item flags to VehicleUpgradeLevels. RaceRun loads the selected car's
CarTypeInfo and passes those levels to the setup producer. Engine tables,
induction, gearbox ratios, tyre coefficients, mass, centre of mass and
struts are read from that car's authored tables.

A bounded audit of the owner's observed Civic selection compared the whole
setup block against original575960 and57F2B0. Separate engine derivative,
integration, rpm publication, cutoff and HUD speed comparisons execute the
original instructions via tools/race_powertrain_native.py. These comparisons
use supplied states and inputs; they do not establish a complete acceleration
trace, tyre/contact schedule, crest trajectory or matching garage equipment
on a separately recorded original game. Those remain runtime acceptance.

## Dependent replay and contact witnesses

The performance replay is regenerated with the corrected producer; its
recording and state/step pins remain separate from the unchanged stock replay.
The native contact generators keep the full57F2B0 result rather than
overwriting it with raw575960. Reproduce them with
tools/race_world_contact_native.py and tools/race_ground_contact_native.py,
then export their output with tools/race_contact_pins.py.
nfsu_race_world_contact_retail compares queue geometry and continuation
states exactly, including grounded pairs. This is controlled contact evidence,
not a complete live race or airborne-trajectory comparison.

The old network level line does not serialize unique-item flags. The bounded
Civic comparison uses those flags disabled; it is not evidence that an
unobserved save inventory has no unique items. The Switch load log now also
prints performance-items once so that the next run can confirm them.
